Learn Survey Design & Sampling with Phoebe

A survey prints one number and one margin of error, and the margin describes only one of the ways the number can be wrong. The others arrive earlier: the question's wording, the list you drew from, who you picked, who answered, and how the answers were weighted. These ten sessions follow one question all the way through: how far is this survey's number from the truth, and which choice put it there? The bench answers it for real. It asks a question of 20,000 adults from the Census 2021 teaching sample of England and Wales, where the true answer is known, and measures every choice against it. Six Analyst sessions are for the people who design, field and weight surveys. Four Leader sessions are for the people who commission them, read them and decide with them.
